Why ES3D came to be

The founder of ES3D. 15 October 2026.

I started on a site that distributes 3D models for printing, and I saw a newly added "Preview in 3D" button. I pressed it and watched the file load from 0% to 100%. That took around eight seconds. The file was the well-known Benchy. It should not have taken that long.

I didn't pay attention to it at the time.

Weeks later, while I was interning at a start-up, they began prototyping 3D models on the homepage. The designs were simple and geometric. The two methods they had found were overlaying two images, or making a video. Both made the page slower to load.

Trying to be helpful, I started looking. I found glTF and the other file formats, and none of them quite did what I wanted. So I set out to fix that.

By the time a working prototype was nearly ready, the internship was over, but the passion was still there. I kept going, mainly to prove to myself that it was possible, and that produced the first prototype of ES3D.

This is by no means a perfect release, but hopefully it is enough to get the initial idea across.
